About MEGA

Middle East Gases Association

MEGA is a non-profit association of companies that produce and distribute industrial and medical gases in the Middle East, together with their equipment and service suppliers. It works with national authorities to bring regulations and safety standards in line with international practice, drawing on the technical work of EIGA and CGA.

Safety and standardsShared technical guidance on production, storage, transport and use of oxygen, nitrogen, argon, hydrogen, CO₂, helium and specialty gases.
Dialogue with authoritiesA single industry voice in discussions with ministries and regulators across the region.
Industry networkInternational, regional and local producers, alongside suppliers of cylinders, cryogenic tanks, valves, compressors and logistics.
